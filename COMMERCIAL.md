## When you need a commercial license

The AGPL requires that if you **convey** TwinRobo, or a program that includes or
is based on it, or let users **interact with a modified version over a network**,
you offer those users its complete corresponding source code under the AGPL.
A commercial license is for when that does not work for you, for example:

- TwinRobo inside a proprietary simulator, robot software stack or product that
  you ship to customers or partners;
- TwinRobo inside a hosted service, SaaS or API that others use;
- a company policy that does not allow AGPL software.

If you are unsure, ask us: we are happy to tell you whether your use needs one.
